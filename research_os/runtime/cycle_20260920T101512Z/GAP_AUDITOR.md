## 2. Strongest evidence

- **V1 provides the strongest negative evidence.** Verified hypothesis features increased frozen August macro log loss from **0.810633 to 0.840193**. The paired improvement interval was entirely negative. July evidence responsiveness passed, so responsiveness was insufficient for effectiveness in this pipeline. This does not establish a new NLP mechanism.
- **The discovery audit weakens intervention sensitivity as a simple ranking signal.** Across 2,000 pooled development/test rows, Pearson correlation with incremental loss was **−0.0334**, Spearman correlation with the validity proxy was **0.0194**, and adjusted Pearson correlation was **−0.0383**. These estimates do not establish independence, exclude nonlinear effects, or constitute confirmation.
- **The broader history favors strong conventional baselines.** Persona features added approximately **0.001 R²**; OW-010B failed its structural gate; Phase II-A did not establish superiority over volume on September or complete bidirectional transfer. None authorizes a graph, latent-strategy, or K-step revival.

## 3. Strongest counterargument

**The proposed “gap” currently compares two numerical quantities whose relationship is not theoretically guaranteed.** The audit’s `F` measures changes in predicted consequence distributions under evidence interventions. `Delta` measures downstream predictive loss relative to M1. Neither directly measures whether a sentence faithfully represents evidence, entails a consequence, or makes an unsupported commitment.

Consequently, low correlation can arise from calibration, feature integration, intervention design, or an inadequate responsiveness measure. B4 degradation does not by itself establish that its natural-language hypotheses were false. Conversely, plausible or responsive language does not establish forecasting value.

The pooled audit, repeated wallets, and outcome-selected illustrative cases further limit inference. There is no demonstrated NLP endpoint separating these branches from explanation evaluation, classification, uncertainty estimation, graph prediction, or anomaly detection.

## 4. Cheapest decisive experiment

**First run a schema-sufficiency and literature gate, requiring no new LLM calls.** This can decisively reject language centrality; passing it would only justify further testing.

1. **Inventory archived language.** On an explicitly declared development slice excluding every frozen test, select cases without consulting outcomes. Check whether actual hypothesis text contains evidence references, temporal scope, conditions, alternatives, or disconfirming observations. If only labels and probability vectors exist, the current artifacts cannot establish an NLP contribution.
2. **Compare language with a lossless template baseline.** Preserve the four consequence dimensions, probabilities, and available as-of facts. Determine whether every proposed scientific endpoint can be computed equally from those fields and deterministic verbalizations. Record any additional semantic commitments and whether they are independently checkable; unsupported extra prose is not automatically useful hypothesis induction.
3. **Demand a branch-specific residual question.** A needs evidence-licensed versus unsupported linguistic commitments; B needs explicitly competing observationally compatible propositions; C needs induced executable consequences beyond fixed labels; D needs genuine ordered updates. Missing artifacts leave those claims untested.
4. **For F, test ordinary uncertainty first.** Any later registered selection experiment must compare against calibrated uncertainty, margin, entropy, activity, and the conventional predictor at matched coverage and measured cost. Use a new untouched temporal holdout with wallet-cluster uncertainty.

Do not optimize prompts, selectors, or thresholds against August/September or the discovery-selected failure cases.

## 5. Stop condition

- **Stop the NLP proposal** if removing natural language while preserving its structured payload preserves every primary scientific endpoint, or if no checkable linguistic construct can be specified.
- **Stop the novelty claim** if the closest existing task already covers the proposed construct, procedure, and evaluation without a demonstrated substantive difference.
- **Keep effectiveness NO-GO** unless a newly registered experiment clears its declared meaningful-effect threshold against the strongest relevant baseline. An imprecise result is inconclusive, not support.
- **Drop identifiability language** if the hypothesis class and observational equivalence cannot be defined. Disagreement or entropy alone is insufficient.
- Preserve all prior NO-GOs. No Temporal GNN, OW-010C, Level 2, or formal K-step expansion follows from this audit.
