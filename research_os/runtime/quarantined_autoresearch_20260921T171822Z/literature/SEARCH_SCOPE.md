# Literature audit scope

Use the supplied preliminary audit and claim matrix as starting context. Attack, rather than
assume, the six candidate novelty claims: action traces to behavioral propositions; explicit
evidence/scope/falsifier; prospective validation; separation of grounding from validity;
claim-level ASSERT/QUALIFY/ABSTAIN; and whether qualification/falsifiability add value beyond
confidence. Do not invent citations; mark unverified items as such. A full fresh systematic review
is not required in this quarantined pass.
